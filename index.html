What is 67 Clicker?

67 Clicker is an incremental idle web game built around the viral “67” meme. Your objective is simple yet deeply satisfying: tap the 67 icon, purchase upgrades to automate point production, and climb from humble single digits into trillions of points.

Is 67 Clicker free to play in the browser?

Yes, 100% free! There are zero upfront costs, zero downloads, and zero microtransactions. You can jump in directly from any modern desktop or mobile browser.

Does the game automatically save my progress?

Yes! The game leverages your browser’s localStorage to save your upgrades, point totals, and achievements automatically. You can close your tab anytime and pick up right where you left off. Just make sure not to clear your browser cache if you want to keep your save file intact.

What is the best upgrade strategy to reach 1 Billion fast?

Follow the 80/20 Passive Rule: Spend 80% of your funds on the highest passive generator tier available (Farms, Mines, Factories), and keep 20% for click multipliers. This ensures your passive income compounds rapidly even while you rest.

How do I troubleshoot if the game fails to load?

If the iframe or game assets fail to display:

  1. Hard refresh your page via Ctrl + F5 (or Cmd + Shift + R on Mac).
  2. Disable any aggressive ad-blockers or script blockers that might prevent iframe execution.
  3. Ensure hardware acceleration is enabled in your browser settings for smooth rendering.
