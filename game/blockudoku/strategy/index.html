Blockudoku Strategy & 3x3 Zone Mastery

Learn how to balance rows, columns, and 3x3 squares simultaneously to hit high scores in Blockudoku.

Quick Answer: Focus on maintaining empty 3x3 squares while setting up line clears. Never allow edges to accumulate awkward single-block crevices.

Core Mechanics

Unlike traditional block puzzles, Blockudoku introduces nine 3x3 sub-grids inspired by Sudoku. Clearing a 3x3 area triggers the same elimination points as clearing a full horizontal or vertical line.

High Score Tips

  • Balance board density evenly across all four quadrants.
  • Don’t rush; time your clears to coincide with tricky piece drops.
