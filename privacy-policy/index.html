Privacy Policy

Last Updated: September 2026

At 67 Clicker, accessible from https://67clicker.com, the privacy of our visitors is of paramount importance to us. This Privacy Policy document contains comprehensive disclosures regarding the types of information collected and recorded by our platform and how we utilize it.

1. Log Files & Analytics

Like most web platforms, we follow standard procedures using server log files and analytics. The information collected includes internet protocol (IP) addresses, browser type, Internet Service Provider (ISP), date/time stamps, referring/exit pages, and click counts. These are not linked to any personally identifiable information and are used solely for analyzing trends, administering the site, and improving gameplay performance.

2. Cookies & Web Beacons

67 Clicker uses cookies to store information including visitors' preferences and the pages visited. This data helps optimize user experience by customizing web content based on visitors' browser types and session preferences.

3. Google DoubleClick DART Cookie & Third-Party Advertising

Google is one of the third-party vendors on our site. It also uses cookies, known as DART cookies, to serve ads to our site visitors based upon their visit to our platform and other websites on the internet.

  • Third-party vendors, including Google, use cookies to serve ads based on a user's prior visits to your website or other websites.
  • Google's use of advertising cookies enables it and its partners to serve ads to users based on their visit to your sites and/or other sites on the Internet.
  • Visitors may opt out of personalized advertising by visiting Google Ads Settings or by visiting www.aboutads.info.

4. Third-Party Game Embeds (iFrames)

Certain games featured on our catalog are delivered via secure sandboxed iframes provided by verified game publishers. These third-party servers may automatically receive your IP address and may use technologies like cookies, local storage, or JavaScript to save your in-game scores and settings.

5. GDPR & CCPA/CPRA Privacy Rights

Under European GDPR and California CCPA/CPRA regulations, users have the right to request access to, rectification of, or erasure of their personal data, as well as the right to restrict or object to certain data processing. If you wish to exercise any of these rights, please reach out to us at our designated contact address.

6. Children's Information (COPPA Compliance)

Protecting children using the internet is an integral priority. We encourage parents and guardians to observe and guide their online activity. 67 Clicker does not knowingly collect any Personal Identifiable Information from children under the age of 13. If you believe your child provided such information on our website, please contact us immediately and we will promptly remove it.

7. Contact Information

For inquiries regarding this Privacy Policy or data protection practices, please contact our team at: privacy@67clicker.com.
